Selected work

Take Time: A Comparative Study of Reading Circles in Galway and Madrid
Forthcoming · Journal of Franco-Irish Studies

A comparative study of two reading communities in Madrid and Galway, exploring how environmental literature can foster slow reading, environmental awareness and community formation. The article develops the concept of BoJoClubs as spaces where slow journalism extends beyond news production into forms of public engagement, allowing small-scale communities to collectively process and cope with the social and interpersonal stresses associated with climate change, including eco-anxiety and news avoidance.

The study connects journalism and communication with environmental education and socially engaged practices, while opening further questions about how journalist-led reading communities might work across different cultural contexts.

The reviewers highlighted the article’s contribution to understanding BoJoClubs as small-scale spaces for environmental dialogue, community formation and public engagement.

Publication: Journal of Franco-Irish Studies · Forthcoming

El Club de Lectura de El País: mediación y transformación periodística
Forthcoming · #PerDebate

This article examines El Club de Lectura de El País as a space where cultural journalism moves beyond traditional forms of gatekeeping and becomes a practice of mediation. It explores how journalists can bring readers closer to books and transform reading into a collective cultural experience.

The article considers the cultural journalist not simply as a transmitter of information, but as a mediator and curator of reading experiences. It also raises broader questions about the relationship between cultural journalism, reading communities, cultural participation and democratic life.

The reviewers highlighted the potential of the article to rethink the cultural journalist as a mediator-curator and to explore how journalism can contribute to shared cultural experiences beyond conventional editorial practices.

Publication: #PerDebate · Forthcoming
